# 3D model review: round 2, Cầu Thượng Cát (`cau-thuong-cat`), 2026-11-27

The user reported that the Thượng Cát model is wrong. Round 1 left it unchanged because sources were thin. This round found the official span figures and the approved design renders. **The current model is wrong in almost every respect:** span arrangement, main span, pylon form, pylon height, deck width and deck height.

Confidence tags: **[C]** confirmed by a source · **[I]** inferred · **[E]** estimated from render or photo proportions.

## 3. Exact pylon geometry (`pylon_custom`)

Pylon-local frame: x is along the bridge (+ toward the main span), y is up from the deck, z is across; the deck edges are at z = ±17.5.

The blade section uses `sweepRect` conventions. For a leg in the y–z plane, `depth_*` runs along the bridge (x) and `width_*` runs across. Each blade is a **wing**: long along the bridge at the base, tapering to a point, thin across.

```json
"pylon_custom": {
  "description": "Dove-wing V pylon: two separate blades rise from one pier, pass just outside the deck edges and lean outward about 15°, curving slightly and tapering to pointed tips; no crossbeam above the deck; a crossbeam under the deck. The centre pylon is about 10 m taller.",
  "legs": [
    {"centerline": [[0,-25,-9],[0,-12,-14],[0,0,-19.5],[0,30,-26.2],[0,60,-35],[0,80,-41.5]],
     "width_base_m": 3.5, "width_top_m": 1.2, "depth_base_m": 12.0, "depth_top_m": 1.5},
    {"centerline": [[0,-25,9],[0,-12,14],[0,0,19.5],[0,30,26.2],[0,60,35],[0,80,41.5]],
     "width_base_m": 3.5, "width_top_m": 1.2, "depth_base_m": 12.0, "depth_top_m": 1.5}
  ],
  "crossbeams_y_m": [-3],
  "anchor_zone": [{"y_from_m": 40, "y_to_m": 77, "leg": 0}, {"y_from_m": 40, "y_to_m": 77, "leg": 1}],
  "struts": [{"a": [0,-25,-9], "b": [0,-25,9], "width": 6.0}],
  "mirror_for_other_pylons": true,
  "reference_images": ["https://tedi.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/CTC_1-scaled.jpg", "https://tedi.vn/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/CTC_2-scaled.jpg", "https://cdn-images.vtv.vn/thumb_w/1200/66349b6076cb4dee98746cf1/2025/10/08/package-media-65167276296726888939424-67085770528280992016896.png"]
}
```

The local y = −25 is the waterline, because `deck_elevation_m` is 25. With `pylon_heights_m: [70, 80, 70]`, G4 scales the side pylons' y > 0 by 70/80 (heights, the anchor zone and the blade tips). The centre pylon is drawn as above. The strut at the waterline stands in for the pier and pile-cap block. Keep it within the pier footprint.

Stays: `cable_planes: 2`, `cable_pattern: "semi_fan"`, `cables_per_side: 11`. The existing `customAnchor` matches each zone to its leg by the sign of the leg's z, so the plane at z < 0 anchors on leg 0. The deck anchors stay at ±17.5, so every stay leans **outward** from the deck to the blade. This matches the TEDI deck-level render.

## 4. Engine gaps

No new engine work is needed. Round 1 covers it: `pylon_custom` handles separate legs with no crown, G4 handles per-pylon heights, and G1 handles `deck_elevation_m`.

Two existing limits, flagged only:
- **Linear taper only.** Real blades have a curved wing outline with the widest point a little above the deck. A linear 12 → 1.5 m taper is an acceptable schematic.
- **Under-construction progress is not modelled.** The engine draws full pylons and 60% cantilevers for every under-construction bridge. In reality the towers are at segments 3–6 of 32 and P30 has no tower yet. That is a global convention; do not change it for this bridge alone.

## 5. Acceptance criteria (what I will check)

Render with `node scripts/render_views.mjs cau-thuong-cat --views side,front,three_quarter,foot,top` plus the custom camera `--az 5 --el 3 --target unit`, and compare with TEDI CTC_1. Add `--az 90 --el 2 --target pylon0`, a view along the deck, and compare with CTC_2.

1. **Side view:**
   - 3 pylons at stations 120, 390 and 660 m in a 780 m unit.
   - Spans read 120 / 270 / 270 / 120; the main-span label is 270 m.
   - The centre pylon is visibly taller (≈80 vs 70 m above deck).
   - No crossbeam above the deck.
2. **Front view:**
   - Each pylon is a **V**: two separate blades from one pier, passing just outside the 35 m deck and leaning outward, tips about 80–85 m apart at the centre pylon.
   - Pointed tips, no head or crown, nothing joining the blades above the deck.
   - A crossbeam under the deck.
3. **Along-deck view (az 90):** blades on both sides of the carriageway leaning away from the road, and stays running from the deck edges up and outward to the blades (cf. CTC_2).
4. **Foot view:** both blades start from one pier block at the waterline. The deck is about 25 m above water.
5. **Labels and stays:**
   - Height labels are ≈95 m (side pylons) and ≈105 m (centre) above water, or 70 / 80 m above deck, depending on the label convention.
   - 11 stays per half-fan per plane in full screen; white pylons; stays visible.
6. **Data:**
   - `main_span_m` 270, `width_m` 35, `deck_width_m` 35, `deck_type` "concrete", `deck_elevation_m` 25.
   - `pylon_heights_m` [70,80,70] above_deck.
   - The `pylons` / `pylons_en` text is updated.
   - Sources include Báo Đấu thầu, TEDI and VOV.
